Digital Twin Health AI

Personalized Preventive Healthcare Simulator

Problem Statement

Modern healthcare is largely reactive. We only treat diseases after symptoms appear. There is a massive gap in preventive modeling that allows users to understand how their daily habits (smoking, BMI, sleep) impact their biological systems before clinical damage occurs.

The Solution

Our platform creates a Biological Digital Twin—a real-time mathematical representation of your core organs. By combining deterministic health logic with advanced LLM reasoning, we provide users with a transparent, "what-if" simulator that maps lifestyle inputs to organ-level risk scores.

Tech Stack

Backend: FastAPI (Asynchronous) Python 3.12 Pydantic v2 (Strict Validation)
AI & ML: Cerebras Inference Llama 3.1 8B (Real-time Reasoning) Deterministic Factor Logic
Persistence: MongoDB Atlas BSON Serialization

System Modules

Reasoning Engine

The "Math Core." It applies clinical weights to habits (like Smoking = 8x Lung Stress) to calculate base scores.

AI Physician (LLM)

Acts as the "Voice of the Twin." It interprets raw numbers into empathetic, scientific narratives using Llama 3.1.

Persistence Layer

Stores simulation history in MongoDB. This allows users to track their "Twin's" health over months of habit changes.

API Gateway

FastAPI routes that handle PDF ingestion, simulation requests, and trend retrieval with sub-second latency.

System Architecture

Our hybrid architecture ensures that AI insights are always grounded in mathematical truth.

[USER INPUT] → [FastAPI Gateway] → [Deterministic Logic Engine]
                                                   ↓
[LLM Reasoning (Cerebras)] ← [Merge & Healing Logic] ← [Numerical Scores (ML)]
        ↓
[MongoDB Storage] → [FINAL REPORT (JSON)] → [3D Dashboard Frontend]

Module Justification

Why Cerebras? We needed ultra-low latency for real-time simulations. Cerebras provides Llama 3.1 at record speeds, making the "Twin" feel alive.

Why MongoDB? Traditional SQL is too rigid for evolving health data. NoSQL allowed as to store complex, nested audit logs of every simulation.

Why Pydantic? In medical AI, precision is non-negotiable. Pydantic ensures the AI output perfectly matches our clinical schemas every single time.
